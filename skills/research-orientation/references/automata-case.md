# Resume a partial extremal result without changing its scope

This is a pedagogical reconstruction from the maintained cerny-direct-attack proof record, not a transcript of private reasoning.

The project studies avoid-or-compress bounds in synchronizing automata. Its current record separates resolved parameter regions, paper arguments, finite controls, and the unresolved region `m >= 6, q >= 3m`. The broader Problem 4 and Černý conjecture are not solved by closing a low-codimension case.

**Available information.** A low-codimension argument had a finite classification and computational exclusions. Some notes describing paper replacements were incomplete.

**Good next decision.** Read the classification and its exact hypotheses. Ask whether trajectory-rank growth, image families, or an explicit short word can eliminate an entire class. Reconstruct the missing steps using those definitions; retain the finite computation as an independent control. Do not merely attach “details unavailable” to a theorem that the available evidence can complete.

**When to check a source.** The compression reduction invokes a published Frankl–Pin refinement. Its alphabet, subset, and connectivity assumptions must match the current theorem; a theorem name alone is insufficient.

**When to submit.** Submit the completed reduction with its exact domain and the independent controls. State the still-open parameter region nearby. A useful paper argument eliminating nine types is worth submitting even though it does not solve every parameter.

**Bad decision.** Rename a restricted search as an exhaustive impossibility result, or label a completed subcase as the full conjecture. Conversely, refusing to submit any rigorous subcase until the entire conjecture is solved wastes genuine progress.
